# Privacy Policy: Prior Inventions

Effective November 27, 2026. Published by Rich Henderson (Doovli), a RevCeleris company.

## The short version

Prior Inventions runs entirely inside your own Claude account. It has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing it reads or stores is sent to Doovli or to Rich Henderson, and neither can see your data.

## What it reads

- Your Claude chats, artifacts, skills and Claude memory, to find what you built and when.
- Your Google Drive, only if you allow it during setup and the Google Drive connector is turned on.

## What it stores, and where

Everything is stored in your own Claude account, on the database of your Prior Inventions page:

- Your settings: your name, initials, time zone, and the company and start date if you enter them.
- Your items: names, short descriptions, dates with links to their records, your In, Out or Discuss decisions, and the Register and Exhibit text.
- Lists of items found but not listed, questions for legal counsel, and the sources searched.
- A revision history of changes to your list.

If you choose to keep the list current, setup saves one short note to your Claude memory. If you turn on updates, setup creates one scheduled task in your Claude account, named "Prior Inventions Updates".

## Outside connections

Prior Inventions downloads nothing. The page, the libraries it uses for Word and PDF downloads, and the task instructions all come inside the plugin, and updates arrive as new plugin versions.

## Sharing

Your Prior Inventions page is private to you. If you share it, the people you share it with can read it but cannot change it.

## Deleting your data

Delete your Prior Inventions page and any design documents it created, delete the "Prior Inventions Updates" scheduled task, remove the memory note if you saved one, and remove the plugin. Nothing remains anywhere else.

## Claude and Anthropic

Your use of Claude itself is covered by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.

## Age

Prior Inventions is not intended for anyone under 18.
