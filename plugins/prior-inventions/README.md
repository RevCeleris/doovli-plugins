# Prior Inventions

Employment and contractor agreements often ask you to list what you built on your own before you start. Prior Inventions finds those items in your Claude chats, artifacts and skills, and in Google Drive if you allow it. It dates each one from a record you can check, and saves them to your own Prior Inventions page. You mark each item In or Out, and nothing goes on the list until you approve it. When you are ready, download the Exhibit for your employer as a Word file or PDF.

## How to use it

After you add the plugin, start a new chat and type "Set up my Prior Inventions." Claude asks six short questions, looks through your work, publishes your Prior Inventions page and sends you the link. You need a paid Claude plan for the monthly update check.

The plugin also includes Keep It Current: when you finish building something new, Claude asks whether to add it to your list and drafts the entry for your approval.

Already have a Prior Inventions page from the Doovli download? Start a new chat and type "Switch my Prior Inventions page to the plugin."

This is not legal advice. Review your list with your own lawyer before you sign anything.

To add it in Claude Code, run: /plugin install prior-inventions --marketplace RevCeleris/doovli-plugins
