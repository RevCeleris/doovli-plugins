# Privacy Policy: Topics Hub with Timeback Machine™

Effective November 26, 2026. Published by Rich Henderson (Doovli).

## The short version

Topics Hub runs entirely inside your own Claude account. It has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing it reads or stores is sent to Doovli or to Rich Henderson, and neither can see your data.

## What it reads

- Your Claude chats, projects, artifacts and the files made or attached in your chats, going back as far as you choose during setup.
- Only the connectors you allow during setup. You can change this later.

## What it stores, and where

Everything is stored in your own Claude account, on the database of your Topics Hub page:

- Your settings: your name, time zone, daily check time, the sources you allowed, and links to your own pages.
- Topic cards: titles, short summaries and links to your chats, artifacts and files.
- Suggestions it makes and your decisions on them (Yes, Not now or Never).

Setup also creates one scheduled task in your Claude account, named "Timeback Machine", which runs the daily check.

## Outside connections

During setup and updates, it downloads the page file and task instructions from a public GitHub repository (github.com/RevCeleris/topics-timeback). These downloads send none of your information.

## Sharing

Your Topics Hub page is private to you. If you share it, the people you share it with can read it but cannot change it.

## Deleting your data

Delete your Topics Hub page and any hub pages it created, delete the "Timeback Machine" scheduled task, and remove the plugin. Nothing remains anywhere else.

## Claude and Anthropic

Your use of Claude itself is covered by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.

## Age

Topics Hub is not intended for anyone under 18.

Any change to this policy is posted here with a new effective date.
