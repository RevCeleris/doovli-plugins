# Career Opportunity Tracker

A free tracker that runs in Claude. Twice a week it finds jobs that match your resume and companies that need your consulting or fractional services, where possible checks each posting is still open on the employer's own hiring system, and logs everything in one place. It drafts tailored resumes, cover letters and outreach, updates itself from your email if you allow it, and keeps your weekly unemployment work search log ready to export.

## How to use it

After you add the plugin, start a new chat and type "Set up my Career Opportunity Tracker." Claude asks four short questions, publishes your tracker, sets up the scheduled searches and gives you the link. You need a paid Claude plan, because the searches run as scheduled tasks. Gmail, HubSpot and Claude in Chrome are optional.

The plugin also includes Apply to a job: with a posting open in Claude in Chrome, say "Add this job to my tracker and tailor my resume," and Claude adds the job, tailors both documents, checks them against your real resume and saves the files.

Already using the tracker from the Doovli download? Start a new chat and type "Switch my Career Opportunity Tracker to the plugin." Your tracker keeps its link and all of its records.

To add it in Claude Code, run: /plugin install career-opportunity-tracker --marketplace RevCeleris/doovli-plugins
