# Privacy Policy: Career Opportunity Tracker

Effective November 27, 2026. Published by Rich Henderson (Doovli), a RevCeleris company.

## The short version

The Career Opportunity Tracker runs entirely inside your own Claude account. It has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing it reads or stores is sent to Doovli or to Rich Henderson, and neither can see your data.

## What it reads

- The resume you upload to your tracker, and your website if you ask it to draft your offer from it.
- Public web pages, to find jobs and prospects and to check that postings are still open.
- Your Gmail, only if you turn on the email check during setup. It reads messages about your applications and prospects. It never sends, deletes or changes an email.
- HubSpot, only if you turn on the HubSpot button, and only to add a deal when you click it.
- A job posting open in Claude in Chrome, only when you ask the Apply to a job skill to use it.

## What it stores, and where

Everything is stored in your own Claude account, on the database of your tracker page: your profiles, settings, offer, jobs, prospects, drafts, notes and your weekly work search log.

Setup creates up to five scheduled tasks in your Claude account, each named "Career Tracker" followed by what it does. You can see and turn them off under Scheduled in Claude.

## Outside connections

The tracker downloads no code. The page, the libraries it uses to read and save files, and the task instructions all come inside the plugin, and updates arrive as new plugin versions. Its searches read public web pages as information only.

## Sharing

Your tracker is private to you. If you share it, the people you share it with can read it but cannot change it.

## Deleting your data

Delete your tracker page, delete the Career Tracker scheduled tasks, and remove the plugin. Files you downloaded stay on your computer until you delete them. Nothing remains anywhere else.

## Claude and Anthropic

Your use of Claude itself is covered by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.

## Age

The Career Opportunity Tracker is not intended for anyone under 18.
